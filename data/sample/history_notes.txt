History Sample Notes — World War II

CAUSES OF WORLD WAR II
1. Treaty of Versailles (1919): Imposed harsh penalties on Germany — war guilt clause,
   reparations of £6.6 billion, loss of territory (e.g., Alsace-Lorraine to France).
2. Rise of totalitarian regimes: Adolf Hitler and the Nazi Party rose to power in Germany
   in 1933. Mussolini led Fascist Italy; militarist leaders dominated Japan.
3. Policy of Appeasement: Britain and France hoped to avoid war by conceding to Hitler's
   demands (e.g., Munich Agreement 1938, allowing annexation of Sudetenland).
4. Failure of the League of Nations: The League lacked enforcement power and the USA
   never joined.
5. German expansionism: Anschluss (annexation of Austria, 1938); invasion of Poland,
   1 September 1939 → Britain and France declared war on Germany 3 September 1939.

KEY EVENTS
- Battle of Britain (1940): RAF successfully defended Britain against Luftwaffe air attacks.
- Operation Barbarossa (June 1941): Germany invaded the Soviet Union, opening the Eastern Front.
- Pearl Harbour (December 1941): Japan attacked the US naval base; USA entered the war.
- D-Day / Operation Overlord (6 June 1944): Allied forces landed in Normandy, France.
- VE Day (8 May 1945): Victory in Europe — Germany surrendered.
- Atomic bombs dropped on Hiroshima (6 Aug 1945) and Nagasaki (9 Aug 1945).
- VJ Day (15 August 1945): Victory over Japan; formal surrender 2 September 1945.

THE HOLOCAUST
The systematic, state-sponsored persecution and murder of six million Jews by the
Nazi regime and its collaborators. Also targeted: Roma, disabled people, political
opponents, LGBTQ+ individuals and others.

CONSEQUENCES
- Approximately 70–85 million deaths (military and civilian).
- Formation of the United Nations (1945) to promote peace and international cooperation.
- Beginning of the Cold War between the USA and the Soviet Union.
- Decolonisation movements accelerated across Asia and Africa.
- Nuremberg Trials (1945–1946): Nazi leaders prosecuted for war crimes and crimes against humanity.
